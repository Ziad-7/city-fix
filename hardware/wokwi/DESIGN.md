## Circuit A: smart bin

Use an ESP32 DevKit board, HC-SR04, DS18B20 with 4.7 kΩ data pull-up, a potentiometer representing battery voltage, SSD1306 128×64 I²C OLED, labeled network and solar/daylight switches, sample-now button, and labeled full/heat/status LEDs with series resistors. Include a charging indicator. A separate solar switch makes the solar state visible rather than implying the battery knob is an actual panel/charger.

Proposed pin map, to be checked against the selected Wokwi board's actual pin labels: ultrasonic TRIG 5/ECHO 18; DS18B20 DQ 4; battery ADC 34; OLED SDA 21/SCL 22; network switch 27; solar switch 26; sample button 25; full LED 13; heat LED 14; status LED 19; charging LED 23. Avoid boot-strap inputs for controls and output-only/ADC mistakes. All switches/buttons have defined pull-ups/pull-downs and debounce. Wire every displayed functional component; do not use unwired decoration as simulated circuitry.

Physical wiring notes must distinguish simulation from real hardware: HC-SR04 normally uses 5 V and its ECHO must be divided/level-shifted before ESP32 (example 1 kΩ top and 2 kΩ bottom resistors); never imply a direct simulated 5 V ECHO wire is safe for a real board. Real Li-ion sensing needs a suitable divider into a safe ADC range, protected battery/charger, and a designed power path. In Wokwi the battery potentiometer is powered at 3.3 V, maps ADC travel to a labeled synthetic 3.0–4.2 V battery estimate, and never represents a direct 4.2 V ADC connection.

Read an odd number of distance samples and use the median; map 100 cm empty / 5 cm full to clamped 0–100% fill. Timeout/disconnected/invalid readings produce a sensor fault, not a fabricated empty/normal bin. DS18B20 asynchronous reads avoid a long UI/network freeze; handle disconnected and conversion-invalid values explicitly. Report sampled distance, temperature, battery, fill, simulated RSSI, firmware version, and unsynced `at:null` unless a genuine clock sync succeeds.

OLED/Serial show **SIMULATOR**, fill/temp/battery, full/heat flags, network, solar/charging state, queue count, transport mode, last ACK/error, and next wake/retry. Large serial status lines supplement the small OLED. Full and heat are independent conditions and may coexist. LEDs include text labels in the diagram/instructions.

Power controls: `solar on/off` and the physical solar switch select synthetic daylight; charging is indicated only when daylight is on and synthetic battery is below 4.15 V. Low battery below 3.4 V increases the demo sampling interval; critical battery below 3.1 V suspends HTTP and shows **LOW POWER**, preserving pending rows and allowing the controls to recover it. Recovered battery resumes delivery. These thresholds are illustrative. No automatic battery movement or energy calculation is needed. Show the firmware state cycle SAMPLE → QUEUED → SEND/RETRY → IDLE/SLEEP. Default cooperative idle makes the demo interactive; optional serial-enabled ESP32 timer deep sleep demonstrates wake/NVS restore but is off by default and clearly interrupts controls until wake.

## Device transport and persistence

Default transport is **LOCAL MOCK — NO HTTP**: firmware simulates a receiver decision, prints the exact proposed payload and explicit mock response, and exercises queue/ACK/settings. Do not imply serial JSON reached the Python dashboard/backend. Network off blocks both local mock delivery and HTTP but continues sampling.

Both projects maintain a small Preferences/NVS durable state record containing next sequence, policy, and unacknowledged payloads (capacity 32). Save append + next sequence as one versioned, checksummed record before attempting delivery; persist acknowledgement deletion and policy together. Retry exact payloads, batch at most 8 readings, delete only known acknowledged sequences, retain every row on malformed/unknown ACK or timeout, and never reset sequence when the queue becomes empty. On capacity exhaustion show **QUEUE FULL**, reject additional samples without consuming a sequence, and keep existing alerts. Never silently overwrite alerts. NVS success/failure must be checked. Document that real ESP32 NVS durability does not establish Wokwi persistence across stopping/recreating a browser simulation; test soft reset/deep sleep separately and report what Wokwi actually retains.

Bin wire shape matches the proposed contract: `seq`, `at`, `fillPct` required; optional `distanceCm`, `tempC`, `batteryV`, `rssi`, `fw`. Endpoint `POST /iot/bins/{id}/readings`, `X-Device-Key`, exact successful `202 {accepted,intervalMin,fullPct,heatC}`; persist and honor returned settings. Default policy 30 minutes, full 80%, heat 60 °C. Fast demo mode visibly scales 30 minutes to 30 seconds without changing wire values; a real-time option uses actual minutes. Equality is active; a below→at/above threshold crossing wakes send immediately, staying above does not repeat immediate sends, below re-arms. Active retry delay is respected; explicit send/network restoration can bypass it. Retry schedule is 1,2,4,8,16 minutes, scaled only in labeled fast mode. Samples and UI remain responsive during outage. No while-loop that waits indefinitely for Wi-Fi/server/sensor.

Serial commands must include `help`, `status`, `sample`, `send`, `network on|off`, `solar on|off`, `mode mock|http`, `endpoint <baseURL>`, `key <disposable-device-key>`, and mock controls for policy plus partial ACK/fail-next. Runtime overrides are explicitly shown as overrides of physical switches; provide `network auto` / `solar auto` to restore switch authority. Do not persist or print keys. HTTP mode is off until explicit runtime endpoint/key configuration. Use Wokwi-GUEST Wi-Fi and finite connection/HTTP timeouts, retain queue during every failed connection. Do not follow redirects with credentials.

For HTTPS, prefer a verified CA configuration and explain how to supply its public certificate. If a disposable-tunnel certificate cannot be validated by a bundled CA, permit an explicit `tls insecure-demo` command only for the mock tunnel, with persistent **TLS UNVERIFIED DEMO** status and disposable keys. Never make insecure TLS the default or call it suitable for real-backend provisioning. A real backend must use normal CA verification and a provisioned key; missing TLS trust fails visibly and preserves the queue.

## Circuit B: collection-hub scale

Separate ESP32, supported Wokwi HX711/load-cell part, SSD1306 OLED, tare button, confirm button, network switch, and status LED. Proposed pins: HX711 DT 32/SCK 33, OLED SDA 21/SCL 22, tare 25, confirm 26, network 27, status LED 19. The primary verified official HX711 documentation: pins are `VCC`, `DT`, `SCK`, `GND`; the 50 kg model maps raw 0–21000, or 420 units/kg. Use that initial simulated conversion while retaining the explicit known-weight calibration flow. Use the supported `HX711` library, stable sampling, finite readiness timeout, and no blocking calibration loop.

Show kilograms, tare/calibration status, selected material, queue and transport state. Material is a serial-configurable supported MaterialId (default `pet_plastic`); pickupId is optional and omitted unless explicitly set. Tare requires an unloaded platform. Calibration accepts a known reference weight, stores the calibration factor in NVS, and clearly says simulated calibration; do not publish an unmeasured accuracy claim. Reject nonfinite/nonpositive known calibration weights, invalid/negative/unstable confirmed weights, and unavailable HX711. Do not auto-send every ADC sample: the keeper presses **Confirm** to append one immutable weighing. Holding/bouncing the button must not create duplicates.

Confirmed payload is `{seq,at,material,kg,pickupId?}` for the proposed future `POST /iot/hubs/{hubId}/weighings`. This delivery confirms weighings to Serial and an explicitly labeled local receiver emulator only. The shared contract does not pin the 202 response body: scale HTTP/backend integration is deferred, and the scale must not fabricate an HTTP acknowledgement or claim backend delivery. Durable queue/seq/calibration, network/offline/retry demonstration, and clear simulation labels follow the bin; explicitly name a simulated ACK as local mock. No BLE-phone or payment integration is implied.

## Free gateway and actual PC reception

Free Wokwi browser Wi-Fi can use its public gateway to an internet-reachable endpoint. It cannot address the user's `127.0.0.1`; private gateway/LAN access must not be a prerequisite. Do not supply fake hosted links, credentials, or paid-plan instructions as the default route.

Provide `python hardware/wokwi/receiver.py` (Windows `py -3`) as an optional receiver-only adapter binding `127.0.0.1:8776`, using runtime `CITYFIX_DEVICE_KEY` and bin ID. Reuse existing `MockReceiver`, `make_mock_server`, and `ReceiverStore` without changing simulator source. Validate/authenticate/idempotently store the bin route; identical retries ACK without new rows, conflicting payloads 409. Keep the adapter standalone rather than starting the existing PC simulator worker or altering its random-key default. Show a readable console summary of latest received measurements/conditions and deduplicated counts, without keys. Do not need a second new web dashboard to prove delivery; any optional read-only status server remains separately bound to loopback. Scale HTTP receipt is deferred.

Optional free HTTPS tunnel instructions can target **only this receiver adapter**, using a user-installed tool such as cloudflared. Example shape: `cloudflared tunnel --url http://127.0.0.1:8776 --http-host-header 127.0.0.1:8776`. Verify supported CLI syntax before publishing. Runtime URL is printed by the user's tunnel; no URL can be supplied in advance. Preserve the receiver's loopback Host allowlist by configuring the tunnel's upstream Host. Expose only the bin POST telemetry route, no control API, files, keys, shell, status dashboard, or existing panel at 8765. Use a fresh disposable key, stop the tunnel after the demo, and explain external gateway/tunnel availability is optional and not verified by local tests. Serial-only mock remains fully usable with no external account/service.

Adapter errors and malformed/oversized bodies are bounded, JSON-only, and credential-free. No permissive CORS or public-interface binding. Device clock may be null. Accept/validate optional bin fields correctly. Do not alter the existing mock receiver's security to make the tunnel work; share pure storage/model helpers where appropriate.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. Bin: distance 100 cm→0%, 5 cm→100%, about 24 cm→80%; temperatures 59→60 °C; show independent full/heat LEDs and equality/re-arm behavior. Invalid distance/DS18B20 produces a fault without unsafe fabricated measurements.
2. Disconnect network; sample normal/full/hot/normal; inspect immutable pending payloads; restore network and see exact ACK deletion. Exercise partial ACK, malformed/foreign ACK, failed response, capped backoff, restart/soft-reset, and 32-row queue capacity. Preserve pending alerts and sequence.
3. Mock policy changes to 1 minute/50%/45 °C apply only through the next valid response; subsequent sample/schedule use them. Fast timing is always labeled. Battery knob crosses 3.4/3.1 V; solar switch changes charging state; recover battery and verify queued delivery. Optional timer deep sleep restores NVS state and is distinct from interactive idle.
4. Scale: zero/tare, apply known supported simulated weight, calibrate, change weight, select material and confirm exactly once. Verify kilogram payload, disconnected/negative/unstable rejection, offline confirmation, sequence/idempotent retry, calibration persistence, and clear unavailable/needs-calibration states.
5. Actual HTTP: local integration tests reach the bin adapter POST route over sockets; optional live Wokwi→public gateway→user tunnel→loopback adapter produces matching deduplicated receiver output. Label this last path unverified if a real public gateway/tunnel run cannot be completed. Scale HTTP is explicitly deferred.
