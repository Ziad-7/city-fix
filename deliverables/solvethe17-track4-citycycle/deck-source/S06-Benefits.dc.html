Who benefits

Citizens
• فرز في البيت بدليل صور وحجز استلام
• تشوف قيمة الخامة، وتديها مجانًا أو بسعر
• البلاغات بتتصلّح وبتتابعها
Sort at home with a picture guide, book a pickup, see what material is worth, give it free or get paid, and watch reports get fixed
Informal collectors

Nearby jobs, verified weights, a record of earnings, pay per kg from a published district price list
Districts

Bins emptied when full, shorter routes, visible dumping hotspots, tonnes diverted measured not estimated
