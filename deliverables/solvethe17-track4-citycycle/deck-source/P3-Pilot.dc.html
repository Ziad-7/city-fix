Proposed development and pilot plan
A Practical Pilot with Measurable Results
Start locally, measure, then expand
Begin with one neighbourhood, participating collectors and a recycling partner.
Use existing bins and hubs; add sensors where they provide clear value.
Demonstrate the full collection-to-recycler journey.
Validate hardware accuracy, connectivity, maintenance and total cost before expansion.
Measure
Every pilot figure is measured on site. No savings or income number is promised before real data.
